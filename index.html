Solar Liberation Front

In this game, the player characters (PCs) are members of a Cell tasked with a clear goal: to cause maximum damage and mayhem to the Human Alliance True Empire, which is ruled by entities from beyond space-time that have enslaved humanity to serve as cheap labor in the construction of a monstrous structure around the Sun called “The Ring.”

To achieve this, the Cell is cursed to use the weapons of its enemies. The PCs acquire printing rights by scanning occult and technological tools recovered from enemy corpses, or scavenge lost technology and mystical artifacts from derelict space juggernauts and long-destroyed cities across the Solar System.

Character creation

  1. Draw 3 tarot cards, choose 2 of them to designate the initial Archetypes, and the third one to the Origin.
  2. Choose Ability die: an ability begins at 2, two at 1, and the last at 0.
  3. Loadout: up to 6 Credits may be spent acquiring equipment.

Basic Mechanics

Minor and Major Arcana

This game uses a tarot deck to determine contextual effects for the players to face, but the Major Arcana are kept separated from the rest of the deck since both card groups are used in different circumstances. Major Arcana are used on character creation and to determine setting-wide effects (mainly related to factions), while the rest of the cards (from now on just Arcana) are used to determine the difficulty of Ability checks.

Ability Checks

When attempting a non-trivial non-impossible action, the Director draws an Arcana, its number determines the difficulty of the action, and its suit may indicate enemy stances (in combat) or additional circumstances (outside combat). The player then rolls a pool of d6, a number of them up to the relevant Ability Score for the type of action attempted, keeping the highest result:

  • Success: if the highest is equal or higher than the Arcana. The action takes effect, its [Total] is equal to the dice result.
  • Mixed Success: if the highest is less than the Arcana. The action takes effect, its [Total] is equal to the dice result, but there is a Cost with a potency equal to Arcana - [Total].
  • Fail: if you roll two or more 1s. This means that the action attempted fails and the character suffers the full Cost as if they would have rolled a 0 on the check.

Critical (Crit) and Crit Effects

If 2 or more dice roll the same result (other than 1s) is considered a Crit, you add every matching die to determine the result, usually achieving a higher [Total], and you get a Crit Effect. Each PC may have a single special Crit effect available from each of these types: 2 of a Kind (two dice with the same result), 3 of a Kind (three dice with the same result), 4 of a Kind (all four dice with the same result), and Flush (four consecutive results). A PC may have access to multiple Crit Effects, in which case must choose during a Sanctuary phase one of each to be available.

Cost

The form it takes is determined by context, but a helpful interpretation guideline is to take it as damage done to the player when in combat, and an equivalent setback, resources or Echo lost outside combat.

Difficulty

The difficulty to resolve a task or to affect a certain enemy/enemy group is determined by drawing an Arcana, usually a 1 to 10 result. If an Ace or Court Card is drawn (Page, Knight, King or Queen) the character making the check may reroll a die for free. For Court Cards its number is considered 10 and an effect is triggered related to the NPC (during combat) and the Suit of the card. For example:

Group Ability checks

When a group effort is needed on an ability check (usually outside combat), a player makes the check while the others may donate Additional dice that may be expended on the roll or to redraw the arcana card. In any case, the Cost is shared by all characters involved.

Additional Dice

On an Ability check players may use additional dice from multiple sources, up to 4d6. These dice may be used instead to reroll dice already rolled on a check. Either way, these additional dice are expended when used.

Abilities

Abilities represent a character’s skills and potential. There are four different Abilities:

  • Dexterity (DEX) reflects physical agility and reflexes.
  • Constitution (CON) measures physical strength and toughness.
  • Intelligence (INT) indicates intellectual capability.
  • Charisma (CHA) represents social and emotional awareness.

Ability Score

Abilities are rated by a value that represents their level of capability, which is added to related checks:

  • 0 – Below human average
  • 1 – Human average
  • 2 – Above human average
  • 3 – Beyond human potential

Secondary stats

► Load is the capacity to equip or install different tools to use in battle. All PCs start with 5+DEX Load slots that can be used to install Prosthetic or Nano enhancements, equip armor, weapons or devices. Usually 1 Load slot is required to equip or install those tools, but this cost can be modified by the Heavy and Light qualities. Unused Load slots can be used as additional dice in combat that are recovered at the end of every round.

► RAM is the capacity to train new capabilities for the character. The PCs start with 5+INT RAM slots, each of them can be used during a Sanctuary phase to temporarily improve a Skill level (a number of RAM slots equal to the new level).

► Echo is the capability to extract power from the nanites in their own blood, some say it is related to cosmic luck or the connection to higher entities. The PCs start with 5+CHA Echo points, each of them can be used during combat to recharge an unloaded weapon as if it were Quicksilver Ammo, or outside combat as additional dice for checks. Echo points used are regained at the end of every combat and Sanctuary phase.

► Health measures a character's damage protection in combat. A character may have Health from multiple sources (like equipment, cybernetics, or from techniques) but the one that provides the most is the one that counts. Everyone has at least 5+CON Health slots, and after spending them they are restored in full at the end of combat. For every point of damage taken a Health slot is spent, when the last one is spent a wound is gained and the Health slots are reset (excess damage is not carried over).

Skills

Skills are areas of knowledge the character is familiar with, they each have a level from 0 to 5. Every PC begins the campaign with three skills at level 1, two at level 2, and one at level 3. Skills affect relevant ability checks where a single die is rolled, adding the skill level to the result. The level also provides access to special effects on criticals:

  • Skill level 2 grants the Two of a Kind crit effect from that Skill.
  • Skill level 3 grants the Three of a Kind crit effect from that Skill.
  • Skill level 4 grants the Four of a Kind crit effect from that Skill.
  • Skill level 5 grants the Flush crit effect from that Skill.

Only one crit effect from each type can be enabled at any time (a single Two of a Kind, a Three of a Kind, etc).

Actions

All combat actions are player-facing: players roll against a difficulty set by the face-down Arcana for the targeted enemy or enemies. After the target enemy is determined its Arcana is revealed, and then the acting player chooses the number of dice to roll on the check. After or before every action taken, the acting character may move up to 5 squares or use a Consumable, then after the action, the targeted enemy may also move up to 5 squares (according to their stance). A PC or NPC that has already moved during the round can only move up to 1 square on the following actions of that same round.

Attack

Make a CON check against a target at close range. Weapon qualities may change the check used and/or the range.

  • Success: The check’s [Total] is the damage done to the target.
  • Mixed Success: As success but you take Arcana - [Total] damage.
  • Fail: You take damage equal to the Arcana.

The target only deals damage if they have a weapon capable of dealing damage at that range, if they do but the weapon needs to be reloaded they reload instead.

Cover

Make a DEX or INT check against the closest enemy.

  • Success: You gain 1 additional die for Resist actions until you move at least 1 square and you may change an equipped weapon for another carried one.
  • Mixed Success: As success but you suffer the enemy effects (determined by their stance), reduced by the [Total].
  • Fail: You suffer the enemy effects (determined by their stance).

Resist

To resist effects from an enemy make a check of any kind and narrate how it helps on the action.

  • Success: You avoid the enemy effects.
  • Mixed Success: You suffer the enemy effects (determined by their stance), reduced by the [Total].
  • Fail: You suffer the enemy effects (determined by their stance).

Combat Procedure

  1. When combat starts a face-down Arcana is drawn for every enemy/enemy group present, and an additional one for their leader (if there is one).
  2. The players take 1 action each, acting first those with more free Load slots, last those with fewer free Load slots. Turn sideways the Arcana used as difficulty in each action. Then for every Arcana not used to determine action difficulty on the round a Resist action is triggered.
  3. Then effects activated at the end of the round take place, all conditions reduce their duration by 1, and a new round begins. This process continues until the combat ends.

Range

Actions are limited by their range, which is determined relative to the target or targets:

  • Close range: Targets must be up to 1 square away from the user.
  • Long range: Targets must be 2+ squares away from the user and within line of sight.

Area effects

An area effect targets up to a number of combatants within range equal to the [Total]/2. The creator chooses which combatants are affected, with the following limits:

  • If the area is in close range, at least one target must be in close range.
  • If the area is in long range, no targets may be in close range.
  • In all cases, the chosen targets must be near each other (no more than 3 squares away from each other).

Line of Sight

A combatant has line of sight respect to another if they can see at least a portion of the target’s body with the size of their head. On a grid line of sight can be determined if any uninterrupted line could be placed from the square of the combatant to the square of their target. The existence of more or less cover is explained mechanically by the variation of Arcana difficulty or the effects of the Cover action.

Wounds & Death

A combatant gets a wound each time they take enough damage to reduce Health to 0, exceeding damage is lost but may cause additional effects depending on the damage type.

For each wound gained, roll a d6: on a 1-4 the wound reduces by 1 an Ability score until healed, on a 5 an equipped item is broken, and on a 6 an installed prosthetic or nano is broken. If a combatant gains 5+ wounds outside combat or ends combat with 5+ wounds, they die.

Damage

There are four damage groups, each of them includes 3 damage types. Excess damage causes an effect depending on its type:

Kinetic

  • Blunt: the affected gains the Diminished condition with duration equal to the exceeding damage after a wound.
  • Piercing: exceeding damage after a wound is taken by another target at close range.
  • Slashing: an additional wound per 10 exceeding damage after a wound.

Chemical

  • Corrosive: an additional equipped item broken per 5 exceeding damage after a wound.
  • Biohazard: the affected gains the Toxic condition with duration equal to the exceeding damage after a wound.
  • Radiation: an additional Ability score wound per 5 exceeding damage after a wound.

Energy

  • Heat: the affected gains the Burned condition with duration equal to the exceeding damage after a wound.
  • Cold: the affected gains the Frozen condition with duration equal to the exceeding damage after a wound.
  • Shock: the affected gains the Stunned condition with duration equal to the exceeding damage after a wound.

Occult

  • Cosmic: the affected gains the Void-Cursed condition with duration equal to the exceeding damage after a wound.
  • Nano: an additional prosthetic or nano broken per 5 exceeding damage after a wound.
  • Psychic: the affected gains the Terrified condition with duration equal to the exceeding damage after a wound.

Conditions

When a condition is applied, it gains a number of duration counters equal to the number of successes from the action that caused it. At the end of each round, remove 1 counter from each condition. When a condition has no counters left, it ends.

  • Burned: The affected receives 3 points of Heat damage at the end of each round.
  • Diminished: checks made by the affected have -1 die, and checks that target the affected gain 1 additional die.
  • Frozen: The affected movement is reduced to 0 squares, and their DR against all Kinetic damage is also reduced to 0.
  • Toxic: Each time the affected receives this condition and at the end of every round, they roll 1d6: on a 1-3 result they take 1d6 points of Biohazard damage.
  • Stunned: checks made by the affected cannot be Critical, and checks that target the affected can add the result of one of the dice rolled to the Total as if it was a Critical.
  • Terrified: The affected can only move while the source of their fear is in line of sight, but cannot approach it.
  • Void-Cursed: At the end of each round, the affected’s max Health is reduced by 1.

Qualities

  • Automatic: May be used to Attack in a long range area, then the user must reload it to be able to use it again.
  • Burst: The user may add +1 die to an Attack action using this weapon, but it gains +1 to its Reload until it is reloaded.
  • Concealable: does not affect NPC reactions while equipped.
  • DR (type) x: Reduce by x all damage received from a damage type. DR from different sources is cumulative.
  • Heavy: Requires 2 Load slots to be Equipped.
  • Holographic: Attack actions with this weapon are made with INT checks.
  • Light: For every 3 items of this type equipped, only 1 Load slot is used.
  • Mystic: Requires to expend 1 Insight to be printed.
  • Nano: Attack actions with this weapon are made with CHA checks.
  • Ranged: Can only be used to attack in long range with a DEX check.
  • Reload (x): After a [Total] of x or less from an Attack action using this weapon, it can no longer be used until reloaded.
  • Space worthy: Provides oxygen, comms, auto-repair sealing, and magnetic boots.
  • Tactical: Can be used to Attack against targets in close or long range with a DEX check, but at close range the check has -1 die.
  • Thrown: This weapon can be used to attack in close range or in long range with -1 die on the check.
  • Two-Handed: Cannot equip another weapon unless it has the Light quality.
  • Versatile (type): The check for actions that use this weapon may be made with any Ability listed in this type.

Resources

Resources are gained collectively and spent individually. Resources do not require load slots to be carried or stored and may not be transferred to another character.

  • Insight: used to train Skills, or to retrain Archetypes.
  • Renown: used to gain access to goods and services, or to buy favors from other factions.

Equipment

In addition to Load slots to equip items, characters have 2 slots to carry unequipped items.

Items

Equipment is divided into five types: Weapons, Armor, Consumables, Accessories, Prosthetics, and Nano.

  • Weapons and Consumables can be equipped or unequipped at any time, with the limit of up to 2 weapons (or a single two-handed one). Armor can only be changed outside of combat (up to 1 helmet, 1 upper, and 1 lower) the same as Accessories (only 1 of each kind), while Prosthetics (up to 1 of each kind) and Nano can be equipped or unequipped only during a Sanctuary phase.
  • Items listed in the equipment tables are level 1 by default, with the exception of Consumables that do not have a level. Level 2+ equipment printing rights may be found as rewards during missions.
  • Level 1 equipment can be purchased when available, its price is always in Renown. Higher-level equipment typically cannot be purchased.

Mods

  • Mods are enhancements that provide extra benefits to weapons or armor. They are installed out of combat, and remain active until replaced or destroyed.
  • Every weapon and every complete set of armor (helmet, upper, and lower armor, even though they are from different types) has 2 Mod slots.
  • Every weapon mod provides the weapon Reload +1, while a modded complete armor set makes its three components lose the Light quality.

Printing rights

  • Each piece of equipment has a price to buy its printing rights. Once it is paid, the PC that bought it may print the piece of equipment once between Sanctuary phases, by paying 1 Renown each time. Printing rights may also be gained by scanning defeated enemies (looting).
  • If more than one copy of a piece of equipment is needed (for example in the case of Consumables), more than one printing right of the same equipment may be bought.
  • If a piece of equipment that is not broken or consumed is printed again the original copý crumbles and becomes unusable.

Manufacturers

  • Each manufacturer sells printing rights for their equipment but they do not sell it to everyone. Those printing rights may also be found randomly by looting.
  • A manufacturer produces 6 different pieces of equipment of a specific level, usually with a thematic modification of base equipment.
  • Weapons from a manufacturer usually have +1 damage per level or an exotic damage type (or a combination of both), while armor from a manufacturer usually provides +1 DR against a different damage type.

Example Manufacturer: “Arc-Protective Systems”

  • Level 1 Equipment.
  • Heavy laser pistol, Laser SMG, Laser Rifle, and Frag grenade: their damage replaced by Shock.
  • Energy field, Battledress: +1 Shock DR.

Factions & The Cell

Factions

  • Factions are groups that share a certain set of values represented by a combination of two archetypes.
  • They have a level that ranges from 1 to 20, representing the maximum level of territory they can control and the maximum level of NPCs in their ranks.
  • Factions have allies and enemies, among which the PCs may be included. It’s in the characters’ best interest to maintain good relations with factions that can grant them access to useful equipment or resources.

The Cell

The Cell is a faction created by the PCs. The campaign begins with the Cell at level 1, this level increases by 1 every time the Cell completes a mission of its level or higher, and it determines the benefits Cell members can unlock:

  • At level 5 and every five levels thereafter members may unlock a +1 to Health.
  • At level 2 and every even level after that members may unlock a +1 to RAM, Load or Echo.

To unlock a benefit a member must train during a Sanctuary phase and pay 10 Insight.

Archetypes

  • Each PC begins with two archetypes representing their training, although they may be changed later via training. To retrain an archetype a PC must have relations with a faction or tutor that may teach it, then during a Sanctuary phase the retraining can be achieved by paying 20 Insight.
  • Each archetype provides a main benefit.

Missions

  • A Mission is a contract taken by the Cell to fulfill an objective related to a faction's interests.
  • Each Mission has a level from 1 to 20 and involves completing an objective within a location, such as retrieving an object or person from a hostile site, clearing it, defeating a specific NPC, or completing a series of tasks. Enemies encountered on the Mission are expected to have a level equal to the Mission’s level.
  • If successful, the PCs earn the contracted reward: 2d6 Renown and Insight, plus 1d6 Renown every even level, and 1d6 Insight for every uneven level after the first. If the Quest fails or is abandoned for another, the faction’s relation worsens.

Sanctuary phase

  • Can only be started by the players at a safe place such as a Base, Ship or Station owned by their PCs.
  • While resting during a Sanctuary phase PCs may get any of the following benefits:
  • Change slots assigned to equipment or enhancements.
  • Print equipment.
  • Train Skills
  • Heal 1 Ability wound.

Non Player Characters (NPCs)

All non player characters share some properties:

  • They have a Threat level from 1 to 5, it determines the number of wounds they need to take to be defeated. Threat 1 NPCs are the least menacing, 3 to 5 of them share the same Arcana each round; Threat 2 are competent enemies, 1 to 3 of them share the same Arcana; every Threat 3 to 5 NPC draw their own Arcana and may be also a Leader (Threat 5 always do). To create a random encounter roll d6, the result is the sum of Threats present.
  • They may belong to a Faction with a level from 1 to 20, it determines the max equipment level they may have (level 1, +1 per five Faction levels).
  • They have Abilities just like PCs, but they do not use them on checks. Instead, NPC Abilities determine what they may have equipped. They have 1 Ability score per Threat level, each of them is an Arcana drawn to determine equipment in the following way: the arcana number (1-10) determines a weapon for that Ability type and a piece of armor (up to 3 pieces of armor). Court Cards are redrawn but provide a Consumable.
  • They have Health (+1 for every 5 Faction levels), but no Load, RAM or Echo secondary stats.
  • They may reload their weapons once per combat per Threat level.
